# **Create - Industrial Skies: Engineering Beyond the Horizon**

## **About Create - Industrial Skies**

**Create - Industrial Skies** is an upcoming progression-focused modpack centered around **Create**, **Create: Aeronautics**, exploration, deep automation, and hints of magic.

Unlike traditional Skyblock modpacks, Industrial Skies takes place in a fully generated **Skylands world**. Inspired by the style of world generation found in **The Aether**, the world consists of naturally generated floating terrain, biomes, and landmasses separated by vast stretches of open sky.

Build factories across the Skylands, develop increasingly advanced automation, explore distant regions, and eventually take your engineering into the skies with **Create: Aeronautics**.

Industrial Skies aims to combine the creativity of Create with a world where transportation, exploration, and logistics become just as important as the factories themselves.

---

## **Planned Features**

- **Create-Centered Gameplay:** Create will serve as the foundation for engineering, automation, and progression.
- **Skylands World Generation:** Explore naturally generated floating terrain rather than a traditional Skyblock void world.
- **Create: Aeronautics:** Build aircraft and airships to explore and transport resources across the Skylands.
- **Deep Automation:** Develop increasingly complex factories and production systems as you progress.
- **Exploration:** Travel between floating regions in search of new resources, locations, and opportunities.
- **Hints of Magic:** Magical elements will complement the pack's industrial and engineering-focused gameplay.
- **Custom Progression:** Recipes, quests, and other systems will be customized as development progresses.
